Our Story

Happiness, served one bite at a time

Ayan began as a small dream with a big heart, a dream to create something fresh, joyful, and made with genuine love. What started with a little stall is our first step towards a much bigger vision: to build a brand that brings happiness through every cup we make.

From choosing fresh ingredients to preparing each order with care, every little detail reflects how much this journey means to us. Ayan is not just about what we serve; it’s about the memories we create, the people who support us, and the dream we’re building one step at a time. From our hands to your heart. ❤️
